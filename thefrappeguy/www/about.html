The Frappe Guy

Frappe, explained in small comic scenes anyone can follow.

What this is

Frappe makes open source business software: ERPNext, Frappe HR, Frappe CRM, Frappe Helpdesk, Frappe Learning, Raven, and the Frappe Framework they are all built on, hosted on Frappe Cloud. There is a lot of it, and the words can get in the way.

So this site tells it as one story. A small company is deciding whether Frappe can run its business, and The Frappe Guy helps the team find out. Each topic is told in short parts that follow on from each other. Every part answers one question with a real example and ends with one thing to remember.

Who makes it

I'm Mauli Mogal, and I work in sales at Frappe. I make The Frappe Guy with the help of AI. I decide what each part teaches, check every fact against frappe.io and approve every panel. Claude, an AI model by Anthropic, helps me write the scripts, compose the panels and build this site.

The characters are never drawn by AI. Every panel is put together from Frappe's own character art.

I read every question that comes in.

This is my own project, not Frappe's official channel. Frappe's documentation and announcements live on frappe.io. If anything here disagrees with frappe.io, frappe.io is right, and I'll fix my page.

The Frappe Guy and the people around him come from Frappe's Character Library and are used with Frappe's permission.

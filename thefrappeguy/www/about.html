What this is

Frappe makes open source business software: ERPNext, Frappe HR, Frappe CRM, Frappe Helpdesk, Frappe Learning, Raven, and the Frappe Framework they are all built on, hosted on Frappe Cloud. There is a lot of it, and the words can get in the way.

So every explainer here is one short scene. Someone asks a question you have probably wondered about. The Frappe Guy answers it with an example from a business you can picture, the real screen names, and what it takes to set up. You leave with one takeaway you can use the same day.

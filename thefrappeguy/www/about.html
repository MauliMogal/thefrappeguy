Frappe, explained in small comic scenes anyone can follow.

What this is

Frappe makes open source business software: ERPNext, Frappe HR, Frappe CRM, Frappe Helpdesk, Frappe Learning, Raven, and the Frappe Framework they are all built on, hosted on Frappe Cloud. There is a lot of it, and the words can get in the way.

So this site tells it as one story. A small company is deciding whether Frappe can run its business, and The Frappe Guy helps the team find out. Each topic is told in short parts that follow on from each other. Every part answers one question with a real example, the real screen names and what it takes to set up, and ends with one thing to remember.

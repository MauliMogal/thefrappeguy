Privacy

Last updated 7 November 2026

This site collects as little as it can. Here is all of it.

Who runs this site

The Frappe Guy is run by Mauli Mogal, who works in sales at Frappe. Contact: hello@thefrappeguy.com.

What the question form collects

Your question, the area you picked, and, only if you give them, your name and email. Your email is used only to reply to you. You decide whether your question may become an explainer; your name is never shown either way.

What the newsletter form collects

Your email address and the page you signed up on. It is used only to send you an email when a new part of the story comes out.

The address is kept on this site, and it is also passed to Kit (kit.com), the service that sends those emails. Kit first sends one email asking you to confirm. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Kit records whether each email is opened and which links in it are clicked. It does this with a small image in the email and by sending links through its own address first. The numbers are used only to see whether the emails are useful.

What happens when you read a page

No advertising, no tracking cookies, no page-view tracking, and no scripts from other companies. Fonts and images load from this site itself.

The software that runs this site, the Frappe Framework, sets a few small cookies on every visit, such as a session id and a language setting. They keep the site working and do not follow you anywhere else.

To stop bots, the site counts sign-ups and questions from each internet address for one hour, then forgets the count. Like any web server, the servers that run this site keep short technical logs of requests.

Where your data is kept

On the server that runs this site, on Frappe Cloud, in Mumbai, India. Questions are kept for 12 months, then deleted. Kit keeps its copy of newsletter addresses under its own privacy policy.

Unsubscribing in Kit stops the emails. Your address stays on this site until you ask for it to be removed: email hello@thefrappeguy.com.

Your choices

Email hello@thefrappeguy.com to see what is held about you, correct it, or have it deleted.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
